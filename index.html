Talk to our team
What does a restaurant marketing agency do?

A restaurant marketing agency helps you attract more guests and keep them coming back. Cover Count Media handles food photography, restaurant video, websites, social media, local SEO, paid ads and review management — plus operations like inventory systems and staff training, so the experience lives up to the marketing.

How much does restaurant marketing cost?

Every restaurant is different, so we scope every engagement around your goals and budget — from a single photo shoot or website build to a full monthly partnership. Book a strategy call and you'll get a clear, written proposal.

Do you only do marketing?

No. Filling seats is half the job. We also build inventory and food-cost systems and train front- and back-of-house teams, so you serve more guests, waste less and turn first-timers into regulars.

Can you help with a new restaurant opening?

Yes. We handle brand, website, Google Business Profile, launch photo and video, pre-opening social campaigns and staff training so you open strong from night one.

How fast will I see results?

Quick wins — a rebuilt Google profile, new photography, better-targeted ads — can show impact within weeks. SEO, content and brand compound over months. You get clear monthly reporting on the numbers that matter.

Do you work with multi-location groups?

Yes. We build brand standards, content systems, training programs and reporting that scale consistently across every location.
